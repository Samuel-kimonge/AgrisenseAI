## Tips for a useful photo
Photograph a single leaf that shows the problem, filling most of the frame. Use daylight, ideally in the shade or on a bright cloudy day, because harsh sun creates shadows and glare. Hold the phone steady and wait for the camera to focus. Place a plain background behind the leaf if you can, such as your hand or a sheet of paper.

## What to avoid
Avoid very dark, blurry or very distant photos. Do not photograph the whole field or a whole plant when you want a leaf assessed. Do not upload photos of other crops or of fruit only, because the leaf model was trained on tomato leaves.

## Why it matters
The AI model looks at colour, spots, patterns and texture. A clear photo of an affected leaf gives more reliable results. When the photo is unclear, the app may say it cannot decide. That is intentional and safer than a wrong guess. When in doubt, take several photos of different leaves and ask a local extension officer to check the plants.
