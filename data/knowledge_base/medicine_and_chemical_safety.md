## Why this app does not give doses
The correct medicine, dose and timing depend on the animal's species, weight, age, pregnancy, health condition and the exact product and formulation. Mistakes can cause treatment failure, poisoning, residues in milk and meat, and drug resistance. AgriSense AI therefore does not recommend veterinary medicines or doses, and does not give pesticide application rates.

## Veterinary medicines
Medicines such as antibiotics and dewormers should be prescribed or advised by a veterinarian or qualified animal health worker after assessing the animal. Follow the product label and the veterinarian's instructions exactly, finish the course as directed, and keep records of treatments.

## Withdrawal periods
After many veterinary medicines, milk and meat cannot be used for human food for a set period called the withdrawal period. It differs by product, so ask the veterinarian or read the label before selling or consuming milk or meat from a treated animal.

## Agricultural chemicals
Use only products registered for the crop and pest in your country. Read the label for rate, timing, protective equipment, and the pre-harvest interval (the minimum days between last application and harvest). Mix and apply carefully, avoid drift, and never store chemicals in food or drink containers.

## Where to get help
Contact a veterinarian, animal health worker, local extension officer or licensed agro-input dealer. For poisoning of a person, seek medical help immediately.
